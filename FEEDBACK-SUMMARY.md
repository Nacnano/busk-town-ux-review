Hi busk.town team,

Some feedback from looking at the site as an ordinary musician reading chords on a phone or iPad. The main request is to make it easier to keep playing without taking a hand off the instrument.

- Keep every chord visible. Long lines can be clipped at the phone edge, and the floating section picker can cover the last chord and lyric. Give the picker its own space and wrap long lines.
- Put the song first. Ads and the first-visit introduction get in the way of the opening music. A playing view would help.
- Add an obvious Pause button. Active Autoscroll shows only percentages; tapping the selected speed pauses it, but that is not explained.
- Explain the tools. Label the phone Tools icon, rename “True” to “Original chords”, and say why a disabled switch cannot be changed.
- Let players tap a chord for its fingering. The diagrams exist far below the song, so looking one up means losing your place.
- Find artists by familiar names. “carabao” fails while “คาราบาว” works; “นิวจิ๋ว” fails despite songs listed under “NEW JIEW”.
- Help people recover from search. An empty artist search needs a message and “Search all songs”. Browser Back should keep the previous search.
- Show how to start a setlist. The About page promotes free setlists, but the guest journey does not explain where to begin.
- Give Sign up a new-account screen. It currently opens the welcome-back login form.
- Explain what needs internet. “Install app” alone does not tell a player whether tonight’s songs will be available at the venue.

These points come from three agent walkthroughs at phone/tablet screen sizes, not musician interviews or physical-device tests. Logged-in setlists, app installation, offline use and audio were not tested. The earlier claim of broken Thai encoding has been removed; the screenshots do not support it. Live search suggestions, the tested title typo and saved key settings do work.
