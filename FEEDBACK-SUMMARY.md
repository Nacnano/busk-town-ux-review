Hi busk.town team,

Some feedback from looking at the site as an ordinary musician reading chords on a phone or iPad. The main request is to make it easier to keep playing without taking a hand off the instrument.

- Fit long lines to the screen. In รอ, the last Bsus4 initially extends past the phone edge. Scrolling sideways reveals it, but that adds a gesture while playing. The floating section picker also overlaps a chord and lyric.
- Put the song first. A large ad sits above the music. An earlier first-visit capture also shows the introduction covering the opening chords. A playing view would help.
- Add an obvious Pause button. Active Autoscroll shows percentages; tapping the selected speed pauses it, but that is not explained.
- Explain the tools. Label the phone Tools icon and use “Original chords” and “Original key” instead of “True” and “Ori”.
- Let players tap a chord for its fingering. The guide exists far below the song, so looking up a shape means leaving the reading position.
- Find artists by familiar names. “carabao” fails while “คาราบาว” works; “นิวจิ๋ว” fails while “NEW JIEW” works.
- Help people recover from search. An empty artist search needs a message and “Search all songs”. Browser Back should keep the previous query and sorting.
- Show how to start a setlist. The About page promotes free setlists, but its joining invitation sends guests to the dashboard without explaining the first step.
- Give Sign up a new-account heading. It opens a welcome-back form, with LINE registration underneath.
- Explain what installation provides and whether songs need internet at the venue.

The screenshots and actions were rechecked on 7 October 2026. We replaced the wrong-song and mismatched-state evidence, confirmed that horizontal scrolling works, and removed the claim that Section name cannot be switched. The earlier Thai encoding claim remains withdrawn.

These were simulated phone/tablet layouts and evaluator walkthroughs, not musician interviews or physical-device tests. Logged-in setlists, installation, offline use and audio were not tested.
